# Lesson Note: 2026-10-20 (S12) Workplace Replies
Practice: voice call with English Teacher (Grok Bot), short workplace response drills.

## Corrections
- ❌ I didn't know the project was delayed... I will check it out and circle back to you.
  ✅ Oh, I didn't realize the project was delayed. Thanks for the heads-up. I'll look into it and circle back with you.
- ❌ I will make sure the presentation become more precise.
  ✅ Sure, I'll make the presentation more concise and go through it carefully. (it + becomes; for slides, "concise" fits better than "precise")

## Useful phrases
- Thanks for the heads-up. 謝謝你提醒我。
- I'll look into it. 我會去了解一下。
- I'll circle back with you. 我之後再回覆你。
- go through it carefully 仔細看過一遍

## Speaking habit
- Restarting phrases ("check, check it out", "become, become"). Finish the sentence in one go.
