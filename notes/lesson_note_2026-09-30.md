# Grammar, Usage & Pronunciation

## Grammar

### 1. Past tense when describing completed experience

**Original:**  
> "I major accounting during my undergraduate"

**Corrected:**  
"I majored in accounting as an undergraduate."

**Rule:** Use the simple past for a completed degree or past experience.

**Drill examples:**
- "I worked in audit for three years."
- "I completed two internships during my master's program."

### 2. Singular/plural agreement

**Original:**  
> "seven company"

**Corrected:**  
"seven companies"

**Rule:** Use a plural count noun after numbers greater than one.

**Drill examples:**
- "I worked with seven companies."
- "I reviewed several manufacturing clients."

### 3. Experience as an uncountable noun

**Original:**  
> "these two experience strengthen my ... skills"

**Corrected:**  
"These two experiences strengthened my analytical and financial modeling skills."

**Rule:** Use *experiences* for separate roles/events; use *experience* for accumulated professional background.

**Drill examples:**
- "Those experiences gave me broader exposure."
- "I have three years of audit experience."
