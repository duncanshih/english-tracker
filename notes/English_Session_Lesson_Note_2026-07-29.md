**Associate:** Morning. It's not too crazy in here yet. You have any trouble getting over here?

## Communication Review

### Conversation Timeline

Greeting → Traffic → Calm morning → Interview role → Why this role → Graduation → Conversation ends

### 1. Good handoff at the beginning

Opening:

> "No, it's quite smooth. What about you?"

你沒有只回答交通，而是立刻把問題丟回去，這是很自然的 small talk 節奏，也是整段最好的 conversation handoff。

### 2. Several answers stopped after one piece of information

最明顯的是：

> "Because I just graduated."

這回答是真實，但資訊量太少。

對方如果不是刻意幫你延伸，話題很容易結束。

例如加入一點背景：

> "Because I just graduated, and I wanted to start my career in public accounting."

只增加一句，對方就有更多地方可以接。

### 3. Missed opportunity to develop the topic

當對方說：

> "Feels like a big step, huh?"

你回答：

> "Yeah. It's a big step for me."

回答沒有錯，但沒有提供新的內容。

例如：

> "Yeah. It's exciting, but I'm definitely a little nervous."

或：

> "Yeah. I've been looking forward to starting full-time."

都能自然延續。

### 4. Good communication repair

有兩次沒有聽懂：

> "I'm sorry, what did you say?"

以及：

> "Can you repeat again?"

你直接要求對方重複，而不是猜測內容。

這讓溝通保持順利。

其中更自然的說法會是：

- Sorry, could you say that again?
- Sorry, I didn't catch that.
- Could you repeat that?

## Grammar, Usage & Pronunciation

### Grammar

#### Pattern

**Original**

> I'm gonna interview for a junior associate.

**Better**

> I'm interviewing for a junior associate position.

或：

> I have an interview for a junior associate position.

**Rule**

Interview 通常不用作「去面試某職位」的這種搭配。

**Drills**

- I'm interviewing for an audit associate position.
- I have an interview for a valuation analyst role.

### Usage

#### Pattern 1

**Original**

> It's quite smooth.

**Natural**

> It was pretty smooth.

或：

> The drive was pretty smooth.

**Why**

英語通常描述的是 **the drive** 或 **traffic**，而不是直接說 "it's smooth"。

#### Pattern 2

**Original**

> Can you repeat again?

**Natural**

> Could you say that again?

**Alternatives**

- Could you repeat that?
- Sorry, I didn't catch that.

### Pronunciation

No pronunciation evidence in the text.

## Next Session Goal

### Live Target

**Add one concrete detail before handing the conversation back.**

每次回答後，至少再補一句具體資訊（原因、感受、例子），再把話題交回去。

### Offline Drills

1. 練習把一句回答延伸成兩句。
2. 練習 "Sorry, I didn't catch that." 與 "Could you say that again?"。
3. 練習回答 "Why are you interested?" 時，固定加入「原因 + 一句延伸」。
